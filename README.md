## 📋 Overview

**zielzeit-scalable-capital** is a macOS menu bar app that shows you a projection of when your Scalable Capital portfolio will reach your financial goal. It's a read-only tool that works with the official Scalable CLI, so you can monitor your investments without any risk.

## ✨ Features

- **Menu Bar Display** – See your portfolio projection directly from the macOS menu bar, no need to open a separate window.
- **Goal Projection** – Enter your target amount and the app calculates when your portfolio will reach it based on your current holdings and assumed growth rate.
- **Swift Charts Integration** – Visualize your portfolio growth over time with beautiful, interactive charts.
- **Read-Only Access** – The app only reads your portfolio data using the official Scalable CLI, so your investments are safe.
- **Automatic Updates** – Your portfolio data is refreshed automatically at a configurable interval.
- **Personal Finance Tracking** – Keep an eye on your FIRE (Financial Independence, Retire Early) journey.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites

- **macOS** – This app is designed for macOS 12 or later.
- **Scalable Capital Account** – You need a Scalable Capital brokerage account.
- **Scalable CLI** – The official command-line tool from Scalable Capital must be installed. You can download it from the [Scalable Capital website](https://www.scalable.capital/).

### Installation

1. Visit the [download link](https://github.com/Fabrianneduodecimal526/zielzeit-scalable-capital/releases) to get the latest release.
2. Open the downloaded file and drag the app to your Applications folder.
3. Launch the app from your Applications folder or Spotlight.

### Setup

1. After launching, the app will appear in your menu bar as a small icon.
2. Click the icon and select "Preferences" to configure your Scalable CLI credentials.
3. Enter your target financial goal.
4. Choose an assumed annual growth rate (e.g., 7% for typical stock market returns).
5. Set your desired refresh interval.
6. Click "Save" and the app will start showing your portfolio projection.

## 🖥️ Usage

Once set up, the menu bar icon will display a summary of your portfolio. Click the icon to see:

- Current portfolio value
- Projected growth chart
- Estimated date to reach your goal
- Progress percentage toward your goal

You can also:

- **Refresh manually** – Click the menu bar icon and select "Refresh Now."
- **Change goal** – Update your target amount anytime from Preferences.
- **View detailed chart** – Click "Show Chart" to see a full-size interactive chart of your projected growth.

## ⚙️ Configuration

The app stores your preferences in a local configuration file. You can adjust:

- **Goal amount** – Your target portfolio value.
- **Growth rate** – Expected annual return percentage.
- **Refresh interval** – How often to fetch new data from Scalable Capital.
- **Currency** – Display in EUR or USD.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

### App Doesn't Launch
- Ensure you have macOS 12 or later.
- Right-click the app and select "Open" to bypass Gatekeeper if needed.

### No Data Showing
- Verify your Scalable CLI is installed and configured correctly.
- Check your internet connection.
- Make sure you have a portfolio in your Scalable Capital account.

### Wrong Projection
- The projection is based on historical data and assumed growth rates. It's not financial advice.
- Adjust the growth rate in Preferences to better match your expectations.
